## In short

- **Who I am.** Daniele Nucciarelli, the controller of the data you give me for a proposal or an engagement. For any request, email [info@daniele.nucciarelli.it](mailto:info@daniele.nucciarelli.it).
- **What I do with your data.** I use it to prepare the proposal, do the work, issue invoices and meet tax obligations. I don't use it for advertising or marketing.
- **For how long.** I keep the contract, invoices and correspondence for 10 years after the engagement ends, then delete them. I delete proposals that aren't accepted within 12 months.
- **Your rights.** You can ask me to access, correct or delete your data, within the limits set by law, and you can lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

## Who this notice is for

This notice covers:

- clients who are individuals: private persons, freelancers and sole traders;
- contacts at business clients: owners, legal representatives, employees and contractors named as contacts for the engagement.

If you are a company, please share this notice with the people whose data you give me. Forwarding them the link to this page is enough.

## What data I process and where it comes from

- **Identification and contact data:** first and last name, company name, role, email, phone number, address.
- **Tax and billing data:** tax code, VAT number, registered office or residence, recipient code or certified email (PEC) for Italian e-invoicing, details of payments received.
- **Engagement data:** proposals, contracts, correspondence, meeting notes and project documents, usernames of accounts you create for me on your systems.

I receive this data directly from you or, if you are a contact at a business client, from the company itself. I don't ask for health data or other particularly sensitive data.

## Why I process it and on what legal basis

- **Preparing the proposal and signing the contract.** Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- **Doing the work and managing the relationship** (communications, meetings, deliveries). Legal basis: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- **Invoicing, bookkeeping and tax obligations.** Legal basis: legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).
- **Protecting my rights** in case of non-payment or disputes. Legal basis: my legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

If you are a contact at a business client, the contract is with the company: I process your contact data based on my legitimate interest in managing the relationship with it (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

**Do you have to provide it?** Yes, for the data needed for the contract and the invoice: without it I can't accept or invoice the engagement.

## How long I keep it

- **Proposals not accepted and related correspondence:** within 12 months of the last message.
- **Contract, invoices, accounting records and engagement correspondence:** 10 years after the engagement ends, to meet tax obligations and to protect my rights in case of disputes.
- **Accounts and access to your systems:** when the engagement ends I delete the credentials I hold and ask you to disable the accounts.

When these periods end, I delete the data.

## Who I share it with

I don't sell or share your data. I disclose it only to:

- my accountant, for bookkeeping and tax filings;
- the provider of my e-invoicing software, which processes it on my behalf;
- the Italian Revenue Agency, through its e-invoicing exchange system (Sistema di Interscambio), as required by law;
- my bank, for payments;
- Proton AG (Geneva, Switzerland), which runs my email inbox;
- public authorities, when the law requires it, and legal advisers, only in case of disputes.

Providers that process data on my behalf are bound by a contract (Art. 28 GDPR).

## Transfers outside the European Union

Proton stores data on servers in Switzerland, Germany and Norway. The European Commission recognises Switzerland as providing an adequate level of protection; Germany and Norway apply the GDPR. The other recipients process the data within the European Union.

## Data I process on your behalf

During an engagement I may access personal data you control, such as data about your customers, users or employees in databases, logs or datasets. I process that data only on your behalf and following your instructions, as a processor. In those cases we sign a dedicated agreement (Art. 28 GDPR), which governs that data instead of this notice.

## Your rights

By emailing [info@daniele.nucciarelli.it](mailto:info@daniele.nucciarelli.it) you can ask me at any time to:

- access your data and receive a copy;
- correct or update it;
- delete it, except for data the law requires me to keep, such as invoices;
- restrict its processing;
- object to processing based on my legitimate interest;
- receive the data you gave me for the contract in a commonly used format, or have it sent to another controller (portability).

I reply within one month, free of charge.

If you believe I process your data in breach of the GDPR, you can lodge a complaint with the [Garante per la protezione dei dati personali](https://www.garanteprivacy.it/) or with the supervisory authority of the EU country where you live or work.

## What I don't do

No profiling, no automated decisions about you, and no use of your data for advertising or marketing.

## Changes to this notice

If I change this notice I update the date at the top of the page. If an engagement is ongoing and the change affects how I process your data, I also tell you by email.
