## When you visit the site

**What data.** To receive a page, your browser automatically sends some technical data: IP address, date and time of the request, address of the requested page, browser and operating system type and, if your browser shares it, the page you came from.

**Why, and on what legal basis.** This data is used only to deliver the pages to you and to protect the site from abuse and attacks. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in keeping the site available and secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

**For how long.** The site is static and access logs are turned off: I don't receive or store this data, and I don't use it to find out who you are or what you read. Amazon Web Services, the hosting provider, processes it to deliver the pages and to secure its own infrastructure, under the data processing agreement it has with me.

**Cookies.** The site sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser and loads no scripts, fonts or content from third-party services.

## When you email me

**What data.** Your email address, your name if you include it, the content of your message and any attachments.

**Why, and on what legal basis.** I use it to reply. If you propose a job or a collaboration, I also use it to assess the proposal before any engagement: in that case the legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Otherwise, it is my legitimate interest in replying to people who contact me (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

**For how long.** If no engagement follows, I delete the conversation within 12 months of the last message. If an engagement follows, I keep the related correspondence for 10 years after it ends, to meet tax obligations and to protect my rights in case of disputes. That processing is covered by the [privacy notice for clients](/en/privacy/clients/).

**Is it mandatory?** No, emailing me is your choice, but without your address I can't reply. Please don't send me health data or other particularly sensitive data: I don't need it.

## Who processes data on my behalf

I don't sell or share your data. I rely on two providers, which process it on my behalf as processors (Art. 28 GDPR), under a contract:

- **Amazon Web Services EMEA SARL** (Luxembourg): site hosting and delivery (Amazon S3 and Amazon CloudFront).
- **Proton AG** (Geneva, Switzerland): email inbox.

I disclose data to public authorities only when the law requires it.

## Transfers outside the European Union

- **Website.** The site's files are stored in the AWS Milan region. Pages reach you through the CloudFront network, which I have limited to servers in Europe, the United States, Canada and Israel; each request is handled by the server closest to you. AWS is part of a US group: transfers to the United States are covered by Amazon's certification under the [EU‑U.S. Data Privacy Framework](https://www.dataprivacyframework.gov/) and by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses included in the AWS agreement. Canada and Israel are covered by European Commission adequacy decisions.
- **Email.** Proton stores data on servers in Switzerland, Germany and Norway. The European Commission recognises Switzerland as providing an adequate level of protection; Germany and Norway apply the GDPR.

## Your rights

By emailing [info@daniele.nucciarelli.it](mailto:info@daniele.nucciarelli.it) you can ask me at any time to:

- access your data and receive a copy;
- correct or update it;
- delete it;
- restrict its processing;
- object to processing based on my legitimate interest;
- receive the data you gave me in a commonly used format, or have it sent to another controller (portability).

I reply within one month, free of charge. As I keep no access logs, I hold no information that would let me link browsing data to you.

If you believe I process your data in breach of the GDPR, you can lodge a complaint with the [Garante per la protezione dei dati personali](https://www.garanteprivacy.it/) or with the supervisory authority of the EU country where you live or work.

## What I don't do

No profiling, no automated decisions about you, and no use of your data for advertising or marketing.
